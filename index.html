Who We Are

About Our Church

✠

Debre Mehret Medhane Alem Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church serves the faithful of the Piedmont Triad through worship, teaching and fellowship. We are a center for spiritual growth and for the cultural heritage handed down to us.

We keep the ancient faith and liturgical tradition of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church: the Divine Liturgy (Kidase), the sacraments, the hymns of Saint Yared, and the feasts and fasts of the Church. All are welcome to come, pray and worship with us.

Church Building Fund

Help Us Build the House of God

የበረከት ተካፋይ ይሁኑ፤ የእግዚአብሔርን ቤት ለመሥራት

By God's grace our community has outgrown the space it worships in. We need a church fit for the holy sacraments, a lasting home for worship, community and the generations to come.

We have received the necessary permits and started the groundwork, but we cannot finish without your help. Every gift, large or small, goes directly toward building the church.
